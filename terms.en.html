2. What the app does, and what it does not do

Emycra lets you record your migraine attacks — start, duration, intensity from 0 to 10, pain location, aura and symptoms — together with the acute medication you take, your preventive therapy with each intake, the triggers you recognise and any notes you want to add. From that data the app calculates statistics, a calendar map, the correlation between triggers and attacks and your adherence to the preventive therapy; it shows a widget with migraine-free days and the attacks this month; it sends you a local reminder for your preventive therapy; and it exports your data as JSON, CSV or a PDF report to show your doctor.

Emycra is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, it does not interpret your symptoms, it does not prescribe medication and it does not provide medical advice. It suggests no doses and no changes of therapy, and it contains no validated clinical scores. The counts and charts it shows are elaborations of the data you enter: they can be incomplete or inaccurate, and they depend on how much you write down and how you do it. They are not clinical measurements and they do not replace an examination or a consultation.

Do not change your therapy on the basis of what the app shows without first speaking to a doctor.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Enter truthful data: the statistics and correlations depend on how accurate it is.
  • You decide, together with your doctor, whether and how to change your therapy. The app decides nothing.
  • Protect your device with a passcode and, if you wish, turn on the Face ID lock in the app: your health data lives there.
  • Keep your own copies of anything you export: the app keeps no copy anywhere else, and uninstalling it deletes your data along with it.
